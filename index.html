About me

Balram Shiwakoti

I wear three hats — and I wear them proudly. By profession, I'm a civil engineer. By passion, I'm a stock market investor. And through this blog, I'm a writer trying to make sense of the overlap between building physical things and building financial ones.

I started investing in stocks long before it was fashionable, driven by the same first-principles thinking I use in structural design. The result? A personal philosophy that treats portfolios the way I treat buildings — engineered for resilience, stress-tested, and built to last.

This blog is where I share my honest learnings — what worked, what didn't, and what I'm still figuring out. No fluff, no hype. Just ideas.

"Compound interest is the eighth wonder of the world. Good engineering is what keeps the structure standing long enough to collect it."

— Balram Shiwakoti
