About

Hi, I'm Balram Shiwakoti

📊

I'm a stock market trader, software engineer, and blogger. My work sits at the crossroads of financial markets and technology — I use both to build things that are useful, and I write about everything I learn along the way.

I've spent years studying how markets move, building tools to trade them more systematically, and engineering the software infrastructure that powers those ideas. Trading taught me discipline; engineering taught me to think in systems. Together, they've shaped how I approach every problem.

This blog is my public notebook — equal parts trade breakdowns, technical tutorials, and reflections on the craft of building and thinking clearly.
